CONCEPT PAPER

—

I. Introduction

The proposed system addresses the growing need for efficient data management in academic institutions. With increasing volumes of student records and administrative processes, a digital solution is imperative. 1

This project is developed as part of the BSIT Capstone Program under the supervision of a qualified faculty adviser. It seeks to design, develop, and deploy a working software system within the academic calendar, adhering to the scope approved during pre-defense evaluation.

II. Statement of the Problem

Current manual processes in the institution lead to delays, data inconsistency, and inefficiency. The proposed system addresses:
1. Lack of centralized record management
2. Manual tracking of submissions
3. Absence of real-time monitoring tools

III. Objectives

General: To develop a web-based system that automates and centralizes data management processes for the target institution.

Specific:
1. Design a database schema for efficient data storage
2. Develop a user-friendly interface for authorized users
3. Implement real-time notification and deadline alert features

IV. Scope and Limitations

The system covers core modules outlined in functional requirements. Development is limited to the current semester timeline. Mobile responsiveness is included; native mobile apps are out of scope.

V. Significance of the Study

This project benefits students, faculty, and administrative staff by providing a streamlined digital workflow, reducing paperwork, and improving processing times across the institution.
