I. Introduction

The proposed system aims to address existing institutional challenges through modern information technology. This document presents the conceptual framework, objectives, and scope of the proposed capstone project for AY 2024-2025.

II. Background of the Study

Current institutional processes rely on manual methods that are time-consuming and error-prone. The increasing volume of transactions necessitates an automated, reliable digital solution.

III. Statement of the Problem

The institution currently faces: (1) inefficiency in manual record-keeping; (2) lack of real-time data visibility; (3) high transcription errors; and (4) absence of a centralized information system accessible to all stakeholders.

IV. Objectives

General objective: design, develop, and deploy a web-based management system. Specifically: (1) eliminate manual processes; (2) provide real-time reporting; (3) ensure data integrity; and (4) improve operational efficiency by at least 40%.
