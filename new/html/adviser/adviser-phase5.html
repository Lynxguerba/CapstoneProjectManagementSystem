I. Introduction

The proposed system aims to address existing institutional challenges through the application of modern information technology. This document presents the conceptual framework, objectives, and scope of the proposed capstone project for AY 2024-2025.

II. Background of the Study

Current institutional processes rely on manual methods that are time-consuming and error-prone. The increasing volume of transactions necessitates an automated, reliable digital solution.

III. Statement of the Problem

The institution currently faces: (1) inefficiency in manual record-keeping; (2) lack of real-time data visibility; (3) high transcription errors; and (4) absence of a centralized information system accessible to all stakeholders.

IV. Objectives

The general objective is to design, develop, and deploy a web-based management system. Specifically: (1) eliminate manual processes; (2) provide real-time reporting; (3) ensure data integrity and security; and (4) improve operational efficiency by at least 40%.

V. Scope and Delimitation

The system covers core functional modules as identified during requirements analysis. It is limited to institutional operations and does not include external agency integrations in this version.
